# Privacy Policy for Briefly Local

## 1. Overview
Briefly Local is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension processes webpage content locally on your device to provide automated summarization, language detection, and translation.

## 2. Data Collection and Processing
- **Website Content:** Briefly Local accesses the text content of active webpages solely upon user request to generate summaries and translations.
- **100% On-Device Local Processing:** All text processing, language detection, and summarization are performed entirely on your local machine using Google Chrome's built-in AI APIs (Summarizer API, Translation API, Language Detection API).
- **No External Servers:** No webpage text, personal information, user activity, or analytics are ever transmitted to or stored on external third-party servers.

## 3. Data Sharing and Tracking
Briefly Local does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data or browsing history with third parties.

## 4. Contact
For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official repository or support channel.
